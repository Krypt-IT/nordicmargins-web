From the shelf to the numbers

No installation and no training. Open the app on your phone and start scanning.

1

Scan your items

Hold the barcode up to the camera. The first time you enter the price – after that the item is recognised. Works in walk-in fridges without signal too.

2

Count in minutes

Count per storage area with big buttons. Save the count and download it as PDF or Excel.

3

See what dishes cost

Add your recipes once. The app calculates food cost per plate and compares it with what was actually used – so you see the waste.

ABOUT NORDIC MARGINS

From the kitchen, not the office

I'm Olliver, a head chef in Örebro. Every week I see the same thing: cream, meat and fish get more expensive – but the menu stays the same. Not because anyone is careless, but because nobody has time to do the maths.

That's why I started Nordic Margins and built Marginal – the tool I was missing in my own kitchen. It does the maths for you, with the same price lists and the same daily reality as you.

Per portion

Every dish gets its own number – not an average for the whole kitchen.

Kitchen language

Portions, waste and suppliers. No consultant jargon.

Decisions

You get a list of what to do, not a report to read.

– Olliver, founder
