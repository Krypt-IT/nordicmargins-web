Privacy policy

Effective from 23 November 2026 · På svenska

Who is responsible for your personal data?

Nordic Margins (sole trader) is the data controller for the personal data processed in Marginal.

What we store

Your email address and encrypted password, which restaurant you belong to and your role, and when you accepted the terms. We also store what you enter in the app (items, prices, recipes, counts) and who saved each count. We do not collect payment card details in the app.

Why

To provide the service under our agreement with you (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): logging you in, storing your restaurant's data and letting your staff work in the same stock. We never sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Where the data is stored

Data is stored with Supabase in their data centre in Stockholm, Sweden. The web app is delivered via Vercel. They are our data processors and may only process the data on our behalf.

How long

As long as you have an account. If you ask us to delete your account, your data is deleted within 30 days, except what we must keep by law, such as accounting records if you have paid for the service.

Your rights

You have the right to know what data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict processing and to receive it in a machine-readable format. Contact us at info@nordicmargins.se. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), imy.se.

Cookies

The app uses your browser's local storage to keep you logged in and remember your language. We use no tracking or advertising cookies.

Contact
